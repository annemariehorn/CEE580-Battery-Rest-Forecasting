**Score: 90/100.** Full marks on content; **5/15 on Clarity and Formatting.**

Grader's main point: the details were very vague to an outside reader. Many of the
letters and numbers (cell labels, thresholds, settings) only make sense in the context
of the dataset, and need to be described. Think primarily about how to communicate
effectively.

## What we are doing about it

- Define every label the first time it appears: what B0005–B0018 are, what a
  "discharge measurement" is, what capacity and Ah mean, what the origin and horizon are.
- Say "discharge measurement" rather than bare "cycle" where it could be ambiguous.
- Name the three information settings in words (capacity only / past rest / future rest,
  oracle) rather than (a), (b), (c).
- Give units with every number (mAh, hours, Ah).
- Each figure and table gets a caption that a reader can understand without the text.
- Before submitting, someone outside the group reads it and marks anything unclear.
